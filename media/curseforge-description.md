# EzNotebook

*A notebook for your adventures, where the todo list ticks itself.*

You logged out last night with a plan: finish the Hillsbrad quests, farm 20 Silk Cloth for bandages, run Shadowfang Keep with the guild, then ding 30. Today you log in and the plan is gone. Was it 20 silk or 40? Did you turn in that quest already? Somewhere in Ashenvale there was a cave with a rare spawn, and you can't remember where.

**EzNotebook** is a notebook inside the game: notes and todo lists in folders, with real quest, item and dungeon links. Tasks with a link tick themselves while you play.

## Notes and lists, in folders

- **Folders inside folders**, each with its own colour. Move pages, folders and tasks up or down from their right-click menu.
- **Notes**: click the page and write. Links you put in stay clickable when you read it back.
- **Todo lists**: type a task, press Enter. Tick it by hand, or let it tick itself.
- **Search** every page from the box at the top.
- **Pin a list** to the screen and tick tasks while you play, with the notebook closed.
- **Right-click** any folder, page or task for its options.

## Tasks that tick themselves

Put a link in a task and EzNotebook watches for it:

| Task | Ticks when |
|---|---|
| a quest | you turn it in (or it's already done) |
| a dungeon or raid | you leave it alive with a boss down |
| a dungeon boss | that boss dies |
| an item with a number: "20 [Silk Cloth]" | your bags and bank hold that many |
| "Reach level 30" | you get there |

Adding links is easy: while you type, **Shift-click** a quest, an item or a spell, or **drag an item** onto the box. The **Quest** and **Dungeon** buttons pick one from your quest log or a dungeon list. A small chat line tells you when a task ticks itself (`/nb notify off` to turn it off).

## Daily tasks

Right-click a task to make it **daily**: it unchecks itself at the daily reset, ready for tomorrow. Done one-time tasks clear away at the reset, so your list stays short. A list can keep them instead, if you like to see what you've done.

## Mark spot

Found a good herb route, a hidden cave, the rare that spawns behind the mill? **Mark spot** saves where you stand as a link in your note or task. Click it later and a pin goes on your map to lead you back. (Classic Era doesn't let addons put pins on the map, so you get the zone and coordinates in chat instead.)

## All your characters, or just one

Your notebook is shared by all your characters: plan your alt's leveling from your main. Any folder or page can be kept for **one character only**, and a list can let **each character tick its own** (handy for "Turn in the cloth quest" on every alt).

## Always at hand

- `/todo <task>` adds a task to your **Today** list without opening anything.
- `/nb` opens the notebook. Two key bindings: open the notebook, and add a task to Today.
- A **minimap button**, an **icon above the chat**, and the **addon drawer** in WoW Forever. Click opens the notebook, right-click adds a task. Each can be hidden from the window's Options menu.
- **WoW Forever and Classic Era**, from one download.

## Commands

`/nb` or `/notebook`

| Command | Does |
|---|---|
| `/nb` | Open or close the notebook |
| `/todo <task>` | Add a task to your Today list (`/todo` alone opens it) |
| `/nb new note\|list\|folder <name>` | Make one |
| `/nb find <words>` | Search every page |
| `/nb pin` | Pin the open list to the screen (again to unpin) |
| `/nb notify [on\|off]` | The chat line when a task ticks itself |
| `/nb icon\|minimap\|drawer [on\|off]` | Show or hide the icon above the chat, the minimap button, the drawer entry |
| `/nb reset` | Put the window, icon and pin back in place |
| `/nb help` | List the commands in chat |
