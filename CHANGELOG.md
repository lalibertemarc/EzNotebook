# EzNotebook

## v1.0.0-beta.1

- First beta: a notebook for notes and todo lists, sorted into folders (folders inside folders, with colours).
- Notes: click a page to write; links stay clickable when you read it.
- Links: while typing, Shift-click a quest, item or spell, or drag an item onto the box. The Quest, Dungeon and Here buttons add a quest from your log, a dungeon or raid, or the spot you're standing on.
- Todo lists tick themselves: a quest when you turn it in, a dungeon when you leave it with a boss down (or when the boss you picked dies), "20 [Linen Cloth]" when your bags and bank hold 20, "Reach level 30" when you get there, a place when you walk into it.
- Daily tasks uncheck themselves at the daily reset; done one-time tasks clear away at the reset (a list can keep them instead).
- Everything is shared by your characters; any folder or page can be kept for one character, and a list can let each character tick its own.
- `/todo <task>` adds to your Today list, `/nb` opens the notebook (also a key binding, a launcher icon above the chat, and the addon drawer in WoW Forever). Pin a list to the screen to tick tasks while you play.
- Search every page from the box at the top.
- Works in WoW Forever and Classic Era.
