# EzNotebook

## v1.0.0

First release: a notebook for your adventures, where the todo list ticks itself.

- **Notes and todo lists in folders** (folders inside folders, with colours). Click a page to write; links stay clickable when you read it back. Right-click folders, pages and tasks for their options.
- **Links**: while typing, Shift-click a quest, item or spell, or drag an item onto the box. The Quest and Dungeon buttons add one from your quest log or a dungeon list.
- **Tasks tick themselves**: a quest when you turn it in, a dungeon when you leave it with a boss down (or when the boss you picked dies), "20 [Linen Cloth]" when your bags and bank hold 20, "Reach level 30" when you get there.
- **Mark spot** saves where you stand as a link: click it later for a pin on your map to find your way back (Classic Era has no map pins for addons: you get its coordinates in chat).
- **Daily tasks** uncheck themselves at the daily reset; done one-time tasks clear away at the reset (a list can keep them instead).
- **Shared by all your characters**; any folder or page can be kept for one character, and a list can let each character tick its own.
- `/todo <task>` adds to your Today list, `/nb` opens the notebook. Also two key bindings, a minimap button, an icon above the chat and the addon drawer in WoW Forever: click opens the notebook, right-click adds a task. Each launcher can be hidden from the window's Options menu.
- **Pin a list** to the screen to tick tasks while you play, and **search** every page from the box at the top.
- Works in WoW Forever and Classic Era.

## v1.0.0-beta.2

- **Minimap button.** Click to open the notebook, right-click to add a task to your Today list, drag it around the map's edge.
- **Addon drawer entry** (WoW Forever) now has a tooltip and the same right-click.
- Both can be turned off in the window's Options menu, or with `/nb minimap off` and `/nb drawer off`.
- **Here is now Mark spot**, and it's a bookmark: it saves where you stand as a link, and clicking the link later puts a pin on your map to find your way back (Classic Era has no map pins for addons: you get its coordinates in chat). A task with a marked spot no longer ticks itself when you walk into that zone, which happened just passing through.
- The add-task and search hints stay inside their boxes.
